## Medium and Challenge questions

Every drill is one of two levels. You only find out which in the answer key.

- **Medium:** you spot the relationship and use it in a new situation. It can still take several steps or careful reading.
- **Challenge:** you also have to make a decision: pick between two reasonable approaches, combine several conditions, work backward, decide which explanation fits, or notice whether there is enough information. A Challenge is harder because of the thinking, not because of big numbers or long wording.

Each block of five questions has three Medium and two Challenge questions, mixed in order.

## How to mark an attempt

Mark each guess or skip **before** opening the key. Then use the key to mark the rest Correct or Wrong.

| Mark | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Correct | You chose the right answer and could explain why. |
| Guessed | You were not sure, even if the answer turned out right. |
| Wrong | You committed to an answer and it was wrong. |
| Skipped | You did not answer. |

**Review guesses as well as misses.** A lucky guess is a question you would probably miss next time. For each guess or miss, write one line: what clue you missed or what mistake you made.

### School days

Do the work after school, at whatever time suits you. Plan **45–60 minutes** for ten questions, marking, and a short retry of anything you guessed or missed.

### First two weekends (Nov 21–22 and Nov 28–29)

- **Morning session** and **afternoon session**: the seven fresh blocks for that day, split between the two sessions, with breaks.
- **Separate review session**: go back over every guess and miss from the day.
- Saturdays have four math blocks and three science blocks; Sundays have three math and four science.

### Final weekend (Dec 5–6)

- **Saturday, Dec 5:** the 30 fresh questions as one **60-minute mixed practice session**, then review. Use the mixed order below.
- **Sunday, Dec 6:** finish the remaining 20 fresh questions early in the day (mixed order below), then a shorter session of targeted redos.
- Questions Q26–Q30 in every category are saved for this weekend. Don't open them early.
